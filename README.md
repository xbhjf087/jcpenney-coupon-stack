## How to apply a JCPenney coupon code at checkout

Online, the code box is slightly buried. Add items to your bag, click the cart icon in the top left, and look for the **"More Savings"** section — that's where the promo code goes. Hit Apply and the order total adjusts before you pay.

Three rules worth internalizing:

1. **One promo code per online order.** You can't chain two percent-off codes together.
2. **Different offer types do stack.** JCPenney applies discounts in a set order: the percent-off or dollar-off coupon first, then any applicable rewards, then a free shipping code if it qualifies.
3. **In-store is more flexible.** Physical stores let you combine a coupon with JCPenney Rewards benefits and credit card discounts on the same transaction.

That last point matters more than it sounds. JCPenney's Rewards program has real teeth at the $500 (Gold) and $1,000 (Platinum) annual spend levels — Gold members get coupon passbooks and a pick-your-own-sale-day, which can beat any public code.

## CashPass: the in-house coupon most people misuse

JCPenney's CashPass rewards deserve their own warning label, because the fine print punishes casual use. CashPass comes in fixed denominations — commonly $10 off a $10+ purchase, or a $5 Challenge version — and the rules are strict:

- **Partial use forfeits the rest.** Use a $10 CashPass on an $11 purchase and the leftover value is gone. Buy something at or just above the face value.
- **It expires at 11:59 p.m. Central Time** on the printed date. No extensions, no cash exchange, no reissue.
- **Returns deduct its value proportionally** from your refund, and the CashPass itself isn't reissued.
- **Excluded:** Marketplace items (third-party sellers), gift cards, in-store services, salon gratuity, international orders, and charitable donations. In Puerto Rico, more brands are excluded, including cosmetics and several watch brands.
- **It stacks.** CashPass combines with another coupon and with earned CashPass Rewards — one of the few truly stackable discounts in the ecosystem.
- Each transaction caps at 10 CashPasses at JCPenney.

Because a CashPass is applied before promotional pricing and percent-off coupons (it's calculated against the pre-tax total), the order of operations slightly shrinks what your percent-off code saves. On small orders it's noise; on large ones it's worth noting.
